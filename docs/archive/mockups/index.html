Weeknight Pad Thai

@wok.this.way on Instagram · 0:48

A 20-minute pad thai with a tamarind sauce you can mix ahead. The trick is soaking the noodles in warm water, not boiling.

  • 20 min
  • Serves 2
  • Easy

Your note

Make this Saturday for Zara. Go easy on the chili.

Ingredients

2 servings

    Steps

    1. Soak the noodles in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain. Warm, not boiling, keeps them chewy.

    2. Stir the tamarind paste, fish sauce and palm sugar together until the sugar dissolves.

    3. Sear the shrimp in a hot wok for 1 minute per side. Set aside.

    4. Scramble the eggs with the garlic, then add the noodles and sauce. Toss for 2 minutes.

    5. Return the shrimp, add the sprouts and scallions, and toss for 30 seconds. Finish with peanuts and lime.

    Don't crowd the wok. If you double the recipe, cook it in two batches.
